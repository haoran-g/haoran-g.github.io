How My Projects Connect
COSMOS → SSM

Continuous advancement in secure memory: from RL-based CTR caching to secret-sharing-based encryption, progressively eliminating metadata overhead.

IMCRYPTO → PPIMCE

PPIMCE extends the IMCRYPTO single-core SRAM fabric to a multi-core system that supports both HE and Garbled Circuits in one unified accelerator.

X-Poly → EvaX-Poly

EvaX-Poly provides the noise evaluation framework that validates and guides X-Poly's crossbar design, bridging device physics and cryptographic correctness.

All Layers → VIPER

VIPER unifies all three levels with a fast AMAT-based system model, enabling rapid design-space exploration across device, circuit, and architecture without cycle-accurate simulation.
