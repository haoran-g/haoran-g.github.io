X-Poly
Accelerating Polynomial Modular Multiplication with Crossbar-Based Compute-in-Memory
Published in: IEEE/ACM International Conference on Computer-Aided Design (ICCAD), 2023
Authors: Mengyuan Li, Haoran Geng, Michael Niemier, Xiaobo Sharon Hu  (Co-first author)
Background

Polynomial Modular Multiplication (PMM) is the dominant computational bottleneck in lattice-based cryptography and Homomorphic Encryption (HE), accounting for over 50% of cloud-side HE workloads and over 90% on the edge. While Number Theoretic Transform (NTT) reduces PMM complexity from O(n²) to O(n log n), existing NTT-based crossbar array (XBA) accelerators suffer from large area costs due to twiddle factor storage and complex butterfly computation mappings. No systematic analysis existed comparing NTT vs. direct convolution approaches for XBA architectures.

Design

X-Poly identifies that Conv1D is the superior PMM algorithm for crossbar CIM. Three key insights motivate this: (1) NTT and Conv1D require the same XBA area, but Conv1D's end-to-end complexity on XBA is lower (O(1) vs. O(1)+O(n)+O(1) for NTT+element-mul+INTT); (2) Conv1D's repeated polynomial coefficients enable data reuse schemes unavailable to NTT; (3) Conv1D generates less cumulative noise since the entire computation completes in one XBA pass. X-Poly uses a hierarchical Tile→PE→XBA structure with a novel bit-mapping (BM) technique that groups same-bit weights across PEs, reducing shift-adders from 8 to 2 in a representative example.

X-Poly achieves 3.1×10⁶ PMM operations/s and 200× latency improvement over CPU. Compared to RMNTT (state-of-art XBA NTT accelerator), X-Poly achieves 3.9× better throughput per area by reducing shift-adder footprint by 80%. Against in-SRAM solutions, throughput improves by 11× with 3× better throughput/area.

Conclusion

X-Poly challenges the prevailing assumption that NTT is the best approach for XBA-based PMM acceleration, showing that Conv1D offers a fundamentally better fit for the XBA computing paradigm. The work provides the hardware foundation for PPIMCE and EvaX-Poly.
